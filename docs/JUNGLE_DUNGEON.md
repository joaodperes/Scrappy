# Jungle temple / four-depth mine — implementation and playtest contract

**Branch:** `fix/foundations-room-flow-20261018`
**Milestone:** priority 5, source reconstruction implemented; Chromium/Firefox gameplay QA remains open.

## Layout and floor elevations

The existing **surface perimeter, elevated ruin platforms, boundary walls, jump pads, lifts and switches** are retained. The original center was mostly empty air; this has been filled by editor-compatible `createSlab` objects with **two deliberately excavated entrances** and steel-railed ramps.

| Transition | From | To | Ramp location and direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| West surface entrance | 0 | −7.5 | x=−24, high end z=−19, low end z=7 |
| East surface entrance | 0 | −7.5 | x=24, high end z=19, low end z=−7 |
| Mine level 1 → 2 | −7.5 | −15 | x=0, high z=43, low z=9 |
| Mine level 2 → 3 | −15 | −30 | x=−34, high z=−45, low z=11 |
| Mine level 3 → 4 | −30 | −45 | x=34, high z=45, low z=−11 |

All five ramps are `createRampBox` entities with a non-zero grade, walkable width, and two matching guardrails. The corresponding **upper floor is removed along the slope**; floor material overlaps the high entry and the ramp lands on the lower floor. Adjacent floor slabs overlap slightly (12 cm per side) to reduce Recast/physics seams. The mine levels have perimeter walls, navigable room partitions, half-height cover, collidable ceiling pieces and side-room hazards/optional destructibles. Essential stairs/ramps are not guarded by mandatory destructible doors.

Map geometry lives in `assets/maps/jungle.json`, through the same generic `instantiateFromParams` and editor round-trip pipeline as the other built-in maps. **Do not duplicate any floor or ramp geometry in `levelJungle.js`.** The level module owns lighting, materials and NavMesh setup only.

## Spawn, floor and combat safety

- Surface player spawn remains near `(15, 0, −55)`.
- M1 at **−7.5**, M2 **−15**, M3 **−30**, M4 **−45**.
- Existing deep weapons are on solid floors: Flamethrower `(−10,−15,26)`, Tesla `(10,−15,−26)`, Railgun `(0,−15,0)`, Crossbow `(0,−30,57)`, Rocket `(0,−30,47)`, and Grenade Launcher `(28,−45,57)`.
- The Jungle enemy and PvP spawn lists have been remapped to floor-supported areas outside ramp openings and old imaginary platforms.
- The map metadata sets `fallResetY: −58`, below the deepest legitimate mine floor. Actual falls reset to the level's configured spawn, not the world origin. Other maps keep their existing default.
- Lava is generated by `createLava` with solid collision, and the spike decals remain layered over solid floor. Hazard registration uses the level's existing `hazardZones` array.
- The formerly misplaced southern destructible gate is restored to `z=−51`, not the middle of the surface.
- The misplaced mine barrel at `(9,−6.9,10)` is returned to its own `(−10,−7.04,10)` configured position.

## Lighting and visibility

The mine now has 12 warm point-light positions spanning all four strata (in `js/levelJungle.js`). These are Babylon light instances, not editor props. The existing Pistol/Plasma/etc. Lantern attachment is a camera-mounted spotlight defined in `js/weapons.js`; NVG is a sight-based post-process defined in `js/player.js`. No new special-case lighting disable rules are introduced for lava, spikes or borders.

## Automated structural gates

```sh
node scripts/validate-map-data.cjs
node --test scripts/jungle-dungeon.test.cjs
node --test scripts/*.test.cjs server/*.test.js
node scripts/check-module-imports.cjs
```

The Jungle structural tests check schema and stable IDs; above-ground coverage; all five shafts' supported top/bottom and clear ramp middle; matching rails/slope; the six deep weapon floor positions; collidable roofs, walls and cover on each level; bot/PvP spawn support; connected floor routes between each pair of ramp destinations; and the deep fall-reset/light settings.

These **do not simulate Babylon collisions or the Recast navmesh**. Before closing priority 5, test the following **in Chromium and Firefox**:

1. Walk down and back up both surface entrances; repeat for M1→M2, M2→M3 and M3→M4, without jumping. Observe seams and head clearance. Verify mine level 4 does not trigger the old y=−25 reset.
2. Check that bots can follow all ramps and navigate between chambers without stalling or spawning below the floor. Test flying enemies, high movement speed and Nightmare density.
3. Verify ceilings and rails actually stop rocket jumps/falls, border geometry has no gaps, and lava blocks movement while dealing damage. Test surface and mine spike hazards.
4. Shoot destructible gates and optional underground walls from both sides; ensure they do not interrupt routes that are essential for bot movement. Verify explosions and debris.
5. Retrieve all deep weapon pickups, trigger mine lights, compare darkness with and without the Lantern, and test NVG enemy silhouettes and frame rate.
6. Start TDM/FFA on Jungle and validate human spawn heights and all elevated ruin / underground access.
7. Edit a room wall/ramp in the editor, export, re-import, and confirm matching collision, render and route behavior. Do not mark priority 5 done based on JSON validation alone.

**Open risks:** runtime Recast link generation on tilted ramps; bot navigation across floor-layer seams; non-authoritative multiplayer bots; perf/visibility in dense late waves. No browser playback is claimed by the source-level tests.
